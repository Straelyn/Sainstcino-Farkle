# The Saintscino Farkle

Farkle with **Kingdom Come: Deliverance** rules, played against an on-chain house opponent with
**EVE**. Every roll uses Sui's random beacon, and wins are paid straight to your wallet.

## Playing
1. Open a table's link (or a storage unit whose dApp URL points at one) and connect your wallet.
2. Pick your bet (min, min ×2 or max) and the score to race to: **1,500, 2,000, 3,000 or 4,000**.
3. **Start**: a coin flip decides who goes first.
4. On your turn, click scoring dice to set them aside, then **Keep & roll** the rest or **Keep & bank**.
   No scoring dice on a roll = bust, and the turn's points are lost. Use all six and you roll all six again.
5. When it's the house's turn, press **House's turn**. Its rolls are shown one by one.
6. First to the target wins. A win pays your bet × the table's payout (1.85× by default, stake included).

## Scoring
| Dice | Points |
|---|---|
| Single 1 | 100 |
| Single 5 | 50 |
| Three 1s | 1,000 |
| Three of a kind (2–6) | face × 100 |
| Each extra matching die | doubles it (four 4s = 800, five 4s = 1,600) |
| Straight 1–5 | 500 |
| Straight 2–6 | 750 |
| Straight 1–6 | 1,500 |

## The house opponent
The house keeps its highest-scoring dice each roll and banks at 1,000 points, at 500 with three dice
or fewer left, or at 300 with two or fewer. Against good play it wins about half of all games.

## Running a table
Connect with no `?table=` in the link, then **Create table**, deposit a bankroll and open it. Each game
reserves its possible winnings until it finishes. A game left untouched past the table's timeout can be
finished by anyone: it's played out for both sides and the player is paid if they win.

If a player's in-game panel goes grey on Connect, have them open the link once in a normal browser,
connect there, then go back to the unit.
